Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Your email address and password (hashed) when you register.
  • Expense entries you create, including amounts, categories, and dates.
  • Basic usage data such as login timestamps.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly:

  • To authenticate you and display your expense history.
  • To generate summaries and insights within your account.
  • To diagnose errors and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we never sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use the following third-party services to operate:

  • Hosting providers for server infrastructure.
  • Google Fonts for typography (loaded from Google's CDN).

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We do not share your personal expense data with any of these providers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app's support channel. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly.
